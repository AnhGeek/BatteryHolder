BatteryHolder — Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

BatteryHolder does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers operated by us. The app talks to the battery-monitoring boards you own, over Bluetooth, USB or your own Wi-Fi network, and everything it records is written to private storage on your phone.

Nothing the app records is transmitted to us or to any third party.

This policy explains what BatteryHolder (“the app”) stores, why it asks for each permission, and what control you have. It applies to the BatteryHolder Android app, package com.batteryholder.

What the app stores on your device

All of the following is kept in the app’s private storage area, which other apps cannot read, and all of it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Board reading log

Your boards broadcast a short Bluetooth advertisement each time they wake. When you switch on background logging, the app records one row per broadcast so you can see history from times you were not looking at your phone. Each row contains:

  • the time the broadcast was received;
  • the board’s Bluetooth address and the short name it advertises (for example BH-d08c);
  • the signal strength of the broadcast;
  • the battery voltage and state of charge the board reported;
  • status flags describing the board’s mode.

This is information about your hardware, not about you. The log is capped at roughly 4,000 rows, after which the oldest are discarded.

Settings

Your low-battery alert preferences (whether alerts are on, the voltage threshold and how often you may be reminded) and the calibration and wiring settings you enter for a board.

Wi-Fi network credentials

If you set a board up to report over Wi-Fi, the app asks for your network name and password so that the board can join that network. These are handled differently from everything else, and deliberately so:

  • they are held only in memory while you are setting the board up;
  • they are written into the board’s own storage, either over the USB cable or over an encrypted Bluetooth connection to that board;
  • they are not saved to your phone’s disk by the app, and they are never transmitted off your local network.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or the identifiers advertisers use.
  • It does not determine or record your location. See the note on the location permission below.
  • It does not sell or share information with anyone, because it does not transmit any to begin with.

Network activity

The app uses the network in exactly two ways, both confined to the local network you are connected to:

  • Finding boards. It browses for boards advertising the _batteryholder._tcp service on your Wi-Fi network.
  • Updating firmware. It uploads a firmware image directly to a board’s own address on your network.

The firmware images the app installs are bundled inside the app itself. It does not download them, and it does not contact any server we operate.

Permissions, and why each is needed

BLUETOOTH_CONNECT		 To discover your boards when they wake and to connect to them for live readings, configuration and wireless firmware updates.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Requested only on Android 11 and older, where the operating system required location access before any app could scan for Bluetooth devices at all. The app does not use it to determine where you are. On Android 12 and newer it is not requested: the Bluetooth scan is declared with the neverForLocation flag, which tells the system the app must not be given location information.

RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To resume background logging after you restart your phone, if you had it switched on.

ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
ACCESS_WIFI_STATE

Keeping and deleting your data

The reading log stays on your phone until you remove it. You can:

  • delete an individual board’s log from that board’s page in the app;
  • turn background logging off, which stops new rows being recorded;
  • uninstall the app, which deletes the log, your settings and everything else it has stored.

Because nothing is sent to us, there is no copy of your data anywhere else to request or delete.

Children

BatteryHolder is a tool for working with electronics hardware and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if a future version adds an optional online service — this page will be updated and the date at the top revised before that version is released.
